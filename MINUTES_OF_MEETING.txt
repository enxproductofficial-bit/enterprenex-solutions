1. AGENDA & EXECUTIVE DIRECTIVES DISCUSSED
================================================================================

1.1 SDLC & SYSTEMS ARCHITECTURE FIRST POLICY
- Direct feature development is paused for tomorrow.
- Total focus is mandated on completing full Software Development Life Cycle 
  (SDLC) documentation, system architecture schematics, and Standard Operating 
  Procedures (SOPs).
- Direct production deployments are strictly forbidden. All development must 
  be validated locally and committed to the company GitHub repository.

1.2 ELIMINATION OF FRONTEND CRUD MANAGEMENT BUTTONS
- Frontend buttons that permit general employees to perform direct CRUD 
  (Create, Update, Delete) on sensitive corporate entities must be completely 
  removed.
- General workforce members cannot create/delete accounts, alter roles, approve 
  their own tasks or leaves, or tamper with system logs.
- All modifications must follow formal ticketing, submission, and supervisory 
  review workflows.

1.3 DATABASE CRASH RESILIENCE & ANTI-INJECTION SHIELD
- Implement deep input sanitization across all API endpoints to neutralize 
  NoSQL and SQL operator injections, as well as prototype pollution attacks.
- Deploy rate limiters and error boundaries to prevent DoS crashes and database 
  connection pool exhaustion.

1.4 DEVTOOLS & ZERO-EXPOSURE SECURITY
- Critical logic (such as employee ID generators, password generation, and 
  encryption salts) must NOT exist on the frontend or be inspectable via DevTools.
- All sensitive logic is relocated 100% to the secure backend server.

1.5 TIME-TRACKING & STOPWATCH ANTI-TAMPER SHIELD
- Addressed critical business flaw where staff could leave timers running 
  unmonitored (e.g. while watching YouTube or minimizing tabs).
- Deployed Tab Visibility listeners and a 2-minute inactivity cutoff that 
  automatically pauses the stopwatch.
- Server-side heartbeats every 60s guarantee that only verified active minutes 
  are credited toward sprint velocity.

1.6 TICKETING SYSTEM & PAID RESOURCE REQUEST PROTOCOL
- Integrated a dedicated ticket-raising system on EDWMS for payroll queries, 
  operational issues, and paid software/infrastructure approvals.
- Leaders can submit tickets with cost estimates for Director approval.

1.7 UPCOMING ALL-HANDS MEETING & ONBOARDING DEADLINES
- Team-wide meeting scheduled for TOMORROW at 7:00 PM IST (Google Meet).
- Coordination deadline with Project Manager (Jyoti) set for OCTOBER 25, 2026 
  to finalize new cohort onboarding:
  * Offer letters & Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs)
  * Payroll sheets & payment records
  * Previous intern records & CRM tracking sheets

1.8 OPERATIONAL DISCIPLINE & COMMUNICATION SLA
- Enforce Pomodoro technique for focused sprint blocks.
- Strict 1-hour maximum response turnaround time across phone and messaging 
  during active shift hours.
